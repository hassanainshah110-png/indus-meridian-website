ABOUT IME

Trade begins with a conversation.

Indus Meridian Enterprises is a trading and e-commerce business focused on connecting suppliers, buyers, and international markets.

Our product interests include rice, spices, mangoes, mandarins, leather goods, and animal lick pink salt. We welcome inquiries from importers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and potential business partners.

We also participate in e-commerce activities involving eBay, Amazon, and Etsy in collaboration with ZIELCOM.
